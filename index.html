Biofluid Health Monitor has launched

Meet UX-plor’s new individual health-monitoring app. Some clinically validated biofluid tests may offer a less-invasive way to monitor selected conditions and reduce repeat needle-based sampling. They are not a replacement for biopsy or other testing across all diagnoses; a clinician determines which tests are appropriate. Explore daily readings, trend reviews, and proposed smart-shoe and hearing-aid integrations. This private demo uses mock data; device connections and provider-record sync are not active.
